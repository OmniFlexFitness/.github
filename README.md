## About OmniFlex

**OmniFlex Fitness** is a science-forward fitness and technology brand built on a simple principle: claims should be backed by data, not hype. We design training programs, formulate supplements, and build software that respects the research. Our content debunks misinformation and educates lifters with peer-reviewed evidence from PubMed, NIH, and established sports science organizations.

We are building an ecosystem where training, nutrition, supplementation, and technology converge — all held to the same evidentiary standard.

## What We Build

- **Evidence-Based Educational Content** — Supplement Sundays, Max Out Mondays, OmniFacts, Based or BS, and Fitness Frauds. Every claim cites peer-reviewed research.
- **Scientifically-Formulated Supplements** — Starting with the Zenith pre-workout line. Transparent dosing, clinically-effective ingredients, no label dressing.
- **Fitness Technology** — Native mobile apps, web tools, and internal infrastructure designed to make evidence-based training accessible.
- **Activewear and Merchandise** — Performance-focused apparel with the OmniFlex aesthetic.
- **Internship Program** — 225-hour credit-bearing internships in UX/UI Design, Brand Management, Social Media, and more.
